The club

Who plays here

Break Point is a club for people who play after work. Sixteen courts in Coconut Grove: indoor hard, brushed red clay and covered glass padel, all lit until closing time.

Book a court in under a minute, find a partner at your level, train with a coach who remembers last week. Twenty coaches teach tennis and padel, from first lessons to tournament prep, for adults and juniors.

Nobody rushes you off the court. Stay after the match, cool off on the terrace and come back tomorrow: we open at six on weekdays and at seven on weekends.
