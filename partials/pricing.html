    FAQ

    Answers before you ask

    Book a court
    Can I book without a membership?
    Yes. Pay as you play costs nothing a month: book any free court up to {{fact:payg.windowWord}} days ahead at the drop-in rate.
    How does annual billing work?
    You pay for twelve months upfront and save {{fact:annual}}. Your court hours still reset every month.
    Do unused court hours roll over?
    On Club and Family, up to four unused hours move to the next month. On Weekday they reset.
    Can I pause or cancel?
    Monthly plans cancel any time before the next billing date. Weekday pauses for up to {{fact:weekday.freeze}} a year, Club and Family for up to {{fact:club.freeze}}, for travel or injury.
    What counts as peak time?
    Weekdays from 4 PM until close. Weekends have one flat rate all day.
    Can my partner use my hours?
    On Family, all four players share the hours. On Club and Family, bring two guests a month: they pay their share of the court.
    Do you lend rackets
    Yes. The front desk finds you a racket if you forgot yours, and padel rackets and balls are free to borrow.
    Can juniors play
    Juniors play on every surface. Under twelve, an adult stays on site outside academy hours.
    When do you open and close
    Weekdays {{fact:hours.weekday}}, weekends {{fact:hours.weekend}}. The last booking starts an hour before closing.
    Is there parking
    Yes. The club in Coconut Grove has 80 parking spaces.
    How do I book a coach
    Pick a coach on the coaches page, choose one of their next free hours and book it. Prices vary by coach.
