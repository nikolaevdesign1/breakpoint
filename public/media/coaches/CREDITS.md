# Coach portraits

Stock portraits from Unsplash, used under the Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no permission needed. The people shown are models, not real staff; names and bios on the site are invented for the mockup.

Processing: background removed locally with rembg (u2net_human_seg), scaled to a common head size, converted to grayscale and tone-matched, exported as 720×900 WebP plus a 240×300 thumb. A third-party logo on the shirt in alexander-milevski.webp was retouched out.

Brand marks retouched out (row 145, local OpenCV inpainting, same size and tone): shirt prints in marcus-bell, jordan-hayes, rafael-costa, andres-calderon and kevin-park; cap logos in ethan-brooks and richard-hale; waistband lettering in lena-hoffmann; small chest logos in luca-romano and mateo-rivas.
