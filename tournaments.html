    FAQ

    Answers before you ask

    Do I need to be a member to enter?
    No. Members get the first week to apply, then every open spot goes to guests. Guests pay the same entry fee.
    How do you decide my level?
    By how many years you’ve played regularly and your age group. If you’re between two levels, pick the lower one. The desk can move you before the draw is made.
    When is the draw published?
    Entries close three days before the event. The draw and your first match time arrive by e-mail the evening after.
    Can I get a refund?
    Yes, in full up to 72 hours before the event. After that we refund only if we can fill your spot from the waitlist.
    What if it rains?
    Clay matches move to the indoor hard courts. If those are full, we finish the next day or refund the rounds you didn’t play.
    Do I need a doubles partner?
    Not always. Leave the partner field empty and we’ll pair you with a player at your level.
    What should I bring?
    Your racket, clay shoes for the red courts and water. New balls are at the referee’s desk.
