FAQ

Answers before you ask

How does the free week work?
Apply below and pick a start date. For seven days you play as a full member of the plan you chose. Before the week ends the desk asks if you want to stay. If you don’t, you pay nothing.
Can I book without a membership?
Yes. Pay as you play costs nothing a month: book any free court up to {{fact:payg.windowWord}} days ahead at the drop-in rate.
How does annual billing work?
You pay for twelve months upfront and save {{fact:annual}}. Your court hours still reset every month.
Do unused court hours roll over?
On Club and Family, up to four unused hours move to the next month. On Weekday they reset.
Can I freeze or cancel?
Monthly plans cancel any time before the next billing date. Weekday freezes for up to {{fact:weekday.freeze}} a year, Club and Family for up to {{fact:club.freeze}}, for travel or injury.
Can I switch plans?
Yes, from the next billing date. Moving up in the middle of a month, you pay only the difference.
Who counts as family?
Two adults and up to two juniors under 18 at the same address. Each extra junior is {{fact:price.junior}} a month.
What counts as peak time?
Weekdays from 4 PM until close. Weekends have one flat rate all day.
