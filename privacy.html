Privacy Policy

A short summary of how we handle your details. Questions? Ask the front desk.

  1. 01What we collectThe name, e-mail, phone and message you send through our forms, and the details of a booking or a membership application.
  2. 02Why we use itTo reply to you, confirm a booking or start a membership. We do not sell your details.
  3. 03Who sees itThe front desk and the coaches involved in your booking. Service providers that run the site and payments see only what they need.
  4. 04How long we keep itAs long as you are a member or have an open booking, then only what the law asks us to keep.
  5. 05Your choicesAsk the front desk to see, correct or delete your details at any time.
